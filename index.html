A software developer from Yemen building systems for businesses in a demanding environment: power and internet outages, modest hardware, and users who want something simple that just works.

I handle the whole project: design, code, database, mobile apps, installation and updates after delivery. The technologies in my projects are what each case called for, not the limit of what I work with.
